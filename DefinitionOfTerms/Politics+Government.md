#   Politics + Government

##  "Never give power to anyone that wants it" 

This sentiment is a well-known philosophy, often attributed in various forms to thinkers like Plato, who believed that those who do not crave power are the most suited to hold it.
Here is a breakdown of why this principle is widely held...

-   Corrupting Influence: Power often attracts the worst candidates and corrupts the best, as stated by author Frank Herbert.
- The Problem with Ambition: Those who actively seek power often do so for personal gain or control, rather than for the public good.
- A "Reluctant" Leader: True leadership is often found in those who do not seek authority, but rather accept it out of a sense of responsibility.
- The Philosophy of Service: True power is best utilized by those who prioritize service to others, not dominance

##  Democracy without Leadership

So how do we provide "Democracy without Leadership"?

We pretty much already have the capability to do this, but we need to change our mindset and the way we think about governance.
