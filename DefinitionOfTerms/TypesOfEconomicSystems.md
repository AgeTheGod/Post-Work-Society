#   Types of Economic Systems

A Monetary Economic System is the prevalent Economic Systems worldwide and Capitalism is the prevalent implementation of a Monetary Economic System.

However Money is by no means the only basis for an Economic System. 

In fact throughout history and even in the latter part of the 20th Century other systems have been tried with varying degrees of success.
