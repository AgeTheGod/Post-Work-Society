#  Generational Wealth Reform

Greed and selfishness are deeply ingrained in many "social laws" that they are now considered to be "human nature" and generally inviolate.
As a result and people have a tendency to hoard more than they need and to exploit others for their own gain.

It's no secret that in most societies the mahority of the wealth is owned by a minority o the population.
This is sometimes expressed as
"X percent of the wealth is owned by Y percent of the people" where X is usually some value well over 50% and Y is some value well under 50% e.g. in the UK "90% of the wealth is owned by 5% of the people".
... or as a comparative ratio e.g. "the top X% hold more wealth than the bottom Y%"

Most of this is what could be called "Generational Wealth" because it's not wealth that has been accumulated by an individual from their own endearvours but is mostly wealth that has been hoarded by previous generations (in the case of the UK this might be over centurues) and passed on through some **Tax Avoidance** mechanism to stop the wealth being diluted or redistributed by the rest of Society.

Unfortunately the people that benefit, or will benefit, from Generation Wealth also happened to have accumulated a lot of power & influence as a result of having generational wealth and, let's be honest, they are not likely to give it up easily.

That makes it a significant blocker to implementing anything that is likely to reduce the unfair privileges that group currently have.

Dealing with this issue without having some kind of violent "Peasants Revolt" is a challenge.
