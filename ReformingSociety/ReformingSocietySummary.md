# Reforming Society Summary

Whilst pondering how to create the Post-Work Society a key stage is identifying the "**Blockers to Progress**" and especially the "**Show-Stoppers**" (it's a standard part of any project initiation process).
We can then decide what we CAN DO and, in some cases, MUST DO to mitigate those Blockers to Progress.

Unfortunately most of those blockers are not technical blockers but social blockers inherent in the way that we have currently structured.
Hopefully, just a quick scan through the [Enabling Technologies](../EnablingTechnologies/EnablingTechnologiesSummary) and [Providing Essential Services](../ProvidingEssentialServices/ProvidingEssentialServicesSummary.md) sections will show that we already have the tools need to create a much better society. 

What we are lacking is the will-power to make the necessary changes happen and a clear view of what those changes should be.
It's a complicated subject and "reform" is not a one dimensional thing with a single solution that will solve all our problems.

This is where I think a lot of the ideas being pushed by one group or another currently fall down. An idea such as [Guaranteed Minimum Income](./EconomicReform/GuaranteedMinimumIncome) or [State Owned Enterprises](./EconomicReform/StateOwnedEnterprises) or any of the other proposed economic reforms are not going to solve problems on their own and even in combination won't work without also [Reforming Politics](./DemocraticReform/DemocraticReformSummary) to extend democracy to more people and reduce the influence of "powerful people" and [Social Reform](./SocialReform/SocialReformSummary) to change the way people view their place in society.

All three of these aspects need to be changed concurrently and it's a bit of a rats nest of interrelated topics but we will try to break it down into manageable chunks and look at each one in turn so I've broken it down into...
- [Democratic Reform](DemocraticReform/DemocraticReformSummary)
- [Economic Reform](EconomicReform/EconomicReformSummary)
- [Social Reform](SocialReform/SocialReformSummary)

In addition, based on historical precedence, it's clear that reforming society is a long and laborious process that can take a generation (at least) to complete.
Many of the blockers to progress are often deeply ingrained in the way that we do things (or how we are taught that it works) and privileges are often protected by powerful vested interests that will try to stop any change from happening.

It's also worth noting that the single biggest blocker to progress, which cuts across all of the above, is that we have a tendency to focus on short-term gains rather than long-term benefits.
This  makes it difficult to implement policies that would allow us to transition to a post-work society because the transition itself is a multi-year (possibly multi-decade) activity that will cause a certain amount of pain to different parts of society as it progresses.

Tis last point, on its own, will cause a lot of resistance from some sections of society and the reason why I'm suggesting that we actually need to [National Plan](../GettingThere/NationalPlan) to ensure that we know where we're going and how we'll get there eventually.
