#   Political Party Reform

Given the proposal to move towards Direct Democracy the importance of Political Parties become much less significant in Society.

However that does not mean that Political Parties will not exist because they do have a purpose for debating and proposing improvements to Society.

Given the importance of Political Parties in most modern day Democratic Processes and the ease with which the Political Party can be corrupted by Ineligible Outside Interests then it's important that Political Parties are held to a strict set of standards.

This standard needs to be encapsulated in legislation in the same way that the standard for other kinds of economically active organizations, such as a Corporations or Charity, are defined in law.

We also need to get back to "Government of the people, by the people, for the people" (Lincoln Gettysburg Address best encapsulates it) so...
- Political Parties can only have individuals as members (i.e. no companies, unions, pressure groups, etc.) 
- Only individuals can make political donations to a Political Party or any member of a political party. 
- Donations should also be capped and not tax-deductible
- All donations however small must be authenticated
- All Policy decisions (including the authoring of a Manifesto) should be subject to some kind of Direct Democracy
- Members of a Political Party, especially those in a decision influencing position, must not accept any kind of donation from any source other than the Political Party itself.
