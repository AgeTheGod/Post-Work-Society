#  Electronic Voting

Two thresholds for voting...
- A majority of eligible voters have voted i.e 50% + 1
- A super majority (>60% of the voters) are in favour of the proposal

That actually means that a new proposal could be adopted by 30% + 1 of the eligible voters but does assume that the mahority of voters don't really care one way or the another about most things.

Finally (a thought) is that in a post-work society we could potentially have a situation where the majority of people are not interested in politics and don't want to vote on every issue, but they would still want to have a say in how their society is run and to have access to a government that is responsive to their needs and that can help to protect their rights and interests.

Another thought is that proposals for improving society should start at the local level and eascalate to the national level if they are successful at the local level. This would allow for a more bottom-up approach to governance and would allow for more experimentation and innovation in how we run our societies.

It also ensures that the people making a proposal first have to convince their local community that the proposal is a good idea before it can be escalated to the national level, which would help to ensure that proposals are well thought out and have a good chance of being successful.
