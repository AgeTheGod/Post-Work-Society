# Taxation

Hoarding wealth is a common practice among the rich, but it can have negative consequences for society as a whole. When the wealthy hoard their wealth, they are not investing it in the economy or using it to create jobs. 
This can lead to economic stagnation and increased inequality.

We can even go so far as agrue that hoarding wealth is theft from the rest of society, because the wealthy are taking resources that could be used to improve the lives of everyone and instead are using it to enrich themselves.

Fornms of Taxation include...
- Wealth Tax
- Inheritance Tax
- Sales Tax especially a Luxury Sales Tax
- Land & Property Tax

- **Progressive Taxation** is a tax system where the tax rate increases as the taxable amount increases. This means that the wealthy pay a higher percentage of their income in taxes than the poor

Normally this is applied to Income Tax but it could also be applied to other forms of Taxation as well.

It's also worth considering whether there should be separate Tax Regimes depending on withere the Taxable Legal Entity is a Person or a Corporation, and whether the Taxable Legal Entity is a Resident or a Non-Resident or wholly External Entity.

Basically a Society...
- Should discourage people to hoard wealth, because it is not good for society as a whole.
- Should give preferential treatment to its own members over non-members, because it is not good for a society to be taken advantage of by outsiders.
- Should have a Taxation system that is progressive and that is designed to ensure that the wealthy are paying their fair share of taxes and that is designed to discourage hoarding wealth.
