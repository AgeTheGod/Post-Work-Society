##  Definition Of Terms 

Throughout this work there are a number of fundamental concepts that are repeatedly used but whose meanings may not be clear. 

In most cases this is because the term is an "_umbrella terms_" with no single universally agreed definition that is generally used to group specific variations together into a common classification.
For example, "_Democracy_" is a means of electing people to manage a Society bur there are many, many different ways that democracy has been implemented in different kinds of Society. 

Concepts also tend to evolve over time so that what the term meant when it was originally coined (in some cases centuries ago) is not necessarily what it means nowadays. "_Money_" is an example of this.

Finally, many of these terms are inter-related so the definition may contextually change depending on the definition of some other term. For example, the definition of "_Capitalism_" depends on the definition of "_Money_".

###  Socio-Political Terms

[What is Society](Glossary/WhatISSociety.md) defines society as organized groups of individuals with shared institutions, norms, and cooperative arrangements for mutual benefit.

[Politics+Government](Glossary/Politics+Government.md) discusses political principles, governance structures, and ideas about democratic institutions and the limits of power.
