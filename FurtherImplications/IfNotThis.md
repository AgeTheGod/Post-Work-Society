#   If Not This Then What?

"If Not THis THen What?" was a common question I discussed widely with people of a certain age group I worked with ovedr the years.

When you're getting on a bit in age and you're able to work up until 75 years old it becomes a progressively more important question when deciding whether to carry on withthe daily grind or go off and do something else with what remains opf your life.

However, it's also a critical question when dealing with the idea of a Post Work Society because if the Daily Grind is ot the main time-consumer then what would people do with all the additional time they have?
