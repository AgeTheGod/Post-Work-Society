#   Funding Energy Generation

In the [Sustainable Energy generation](./SustainableEnergy) introduction I mentioned split Energy Management into a three part problem...
1. How to generate Electricity from sustainable sources?
2. How to store Electricity until it is required?
3. How to distribute Electricity to where it is needed?

If we also split the Energy Consumers into three groups - Domestic Consumers, Commercial Consumers and Government Consumers - then it's pretty easy to argue that the way that each group should pay for its Energy can (and should be) different.

So, what we'll look at here is a hybrid commercial model based on a combined public / private funding approach coupled with variable price tariffs for differnt kinds of consumers.
