#   Analysis Overview

One of the benefits of the **Information Age** is that there really is a lot of information and data available about the world around us.

Pretty much every government publishes national statistics about a wide range of topics and there are several other organizations that publish data and analysis.

The only problem is that most of it is online so joining it all together is a bit of a challenge. Consequently, any analysis tends to be approximately correct rather than precisely correct, but that's OK because we are not trying to be precise, we are just trying to get a general answer to general questions. 

In addition data gets out of date very quickly.

So, it's wprth bearing these two points in mind when reading through the topics in this section.
