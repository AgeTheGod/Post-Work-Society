"_If we wish to diminish the love of money which, we are told, is the root of all evil, the first step must be the creation of a system in which everyone has enough and no one has too much._"
: Bertrand Russell

Russell understood that the psychological drive to hoard wealth is often a reaction to living in a zero-sum economy where survival feels like a competition.
By ensuring a solid foundation for every member of society, we can strip money of its power to dictate our behavior. 
When the threat of poverty is removed, the frantic pursuit of excess loses its utility, allowing us to focus on more meaningful human endeavors like art, community, and intellectual growth.
