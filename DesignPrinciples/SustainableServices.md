#   Sustainable Services

"**Sustainable**" means able to keep going at the same rate or level for a long time without running out of resources or causing lasting harm. 
In environmental terms, it means using natural things like water, energy, and soil in a way that saves them for the future.

In the context of defining a Post Work Society, "**Sustainable Services**" are Public Services that can continue to operate for prolonged periods of time (ideally decades) without running out of money, resources, or people to run them.
They balance environmental protection, financial health, and social fairness to stay reliable for decades.

The core features of Sustainable Services are:

|                    |                                                                                                                                 |
|--------------------|---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Eco-friendly       | They use clean energy, produce minimal waste, and conserve natural resources.                                                   |
| Low maintenance    | They are designed to require minimal upkeep and repairs.                                                                        |
| Long-Lived         | They are built to last for many years, reducing the need for frequent replacements.                                             |
| Socially fair      | They are equally affordable, safe, and accessible to everyone in a society removing the need for alternative private provision. |
| Financially stable | They have long-term funding and low maintenance costs so they do not collapse.                                                  |
