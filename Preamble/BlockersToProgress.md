# Blockers Summary

The biggest blockers to creating a Post Work Society are... 

- We have a political and economic system that is based on the idea of work and income and accumulating "wealth". Consequently people are conditioned (through education) to believe that work is necessary for a fulfilling life and that income is the only way to measure a person's contribution to society.
- We seem to like attack (and sometimes kill) each other over the things that we disagree about, which makes it difficult to have a productive conversation about how to create a better future for everyone.
- We have far too many belief systems (i.e. religions) that are based on the idea of "us vs them" (believers vs non-believers) and that if you're not "us" then you're in some way evil and destined for damnation.
- We have a tendency to focus on short-term gains rather than long-term benefits, which makes it difficult to implement policies that would allow us to transition to a post-work society.
- Greed and selfishness are deeply ingrained in human nature and people have a trendency to hoard more than they need and to exploit others for their own gain.

##  Note about Religion

I don't have a problem with religion per se. People need to find reasons for existence, why we are here, and what comes afterwards because otherwise the entire thing is just a big waste of time and energy. 
However I do have a problem with the way that religion is used to justify violence and to control people.
That is not what it's supposed to be about.
