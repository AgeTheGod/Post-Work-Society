# Blockers Summary

When it comes to propsing any kind of radical change then it's important to also understand the various blockers that exist that will stop any change from being successful.

In this section I'm listed what I see as the biggest blockers to implementing significant social and technological changes for the benefit of the many.

In most cases these are blockers inherent in Social Structure rather than Technology limitations. 

##  Political Blockers

We have a political and economic system that is based on the idea of work and income and accumulating "wealth". 
Consequently people are conditioned (through early education) to believe that work is necessary for a fulfilling life and that income is the only way to measure a person's contribution to society.
 
##  Organized Religion

We have far too many belief systems (mostly religions but otgher kinds of belief systems as well) that are based on the idea of "us vs them" (believers vs non-believers) and that if you're not "us" then you're "one of them" and in some way evil and destined for damnation.

I don't have a problem with religion per se. 

People need to find reasons for existence, why we are here, and what comes afterwards because otherwise the entire thing is just a big waste of time and energy. 
However I do have a problem with the way that Organized Religion is used to justify violence and to control people.

That is not what it's supposed to be about.

##  Generational Wealth

Greed and selfishness are deeply ingrained in many "social laws" that they are now considered to be "human nature" and generally inviolate. 
As a result and people have a tendency to hoard more than they need and to exploit others for their own gain.

It's no secret that in most societies the mahority of the wealth is owned by a minority o the population. 
This is sometimes expressed as
"X percent of the wealth is owned by Y percent of the people" where X is usually some value well over 50% and Y is some value well under 50% e.g. in the UK "90% of the wealth is owned by 5% of the people".
 ... or as a comparative ratio e.g. "the top X% hold more wealth than the bottom Y%"

Most of this is what could be called "Generational Wealth" because it's not wealth that has been accumulated by an individual from their own endearvours but is mostly wealth that has been hoarded by previous generations (in the case of the UK this might be over centurues) and passed on through some **Tax Avoidance** mechanism to stop the wealth being diluted or redistributed by the rest of Society. 

Unfortunately the people that benefit, or will benefit, from Generation Wealth also happened to have accumulated a lot of power & influence as a result of having generational wealth and, let's be honest, they are not likely to give it up easily.

That makes it a significant blocker to implementing anything that is likely to reduce the unfair privileges that group currently have. 

Dealing with this issue without having some kind of violent "Peasants Revolt" is a challenge.

##  Vested Interests

I know there will likely be many arguments against some of these conclusions mostly from people that will fall into some group or another that is adversely affected by whatever conclusion I come up with.
For example (from experience working on it) Estate Agents and Surveyors were highly critical of the Home Information Pack legislation not because it was bad but because it effectively reduced the work they would do and hence what they could charge for it.
Both groups actively lobbied the government of the daya (Labour) to kill or water down the legislation and failed in that but did succeed in convincing the Conservatives to revoke it which they immediately did when they returned to power in 2010.

These "vested interests" will always be there to some degree or another and will try to block any kind of change that adversely affects them.
