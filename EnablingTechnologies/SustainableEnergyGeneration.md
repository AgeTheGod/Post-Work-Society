#   Sustainable Energy Generation

Energy generation is a critical enabling technology for the post work society but when we talk about Energy Generation we're really talking about "Electricity" because that's what powers pretty much everything that mopst of us care about.

Fortunately, because of the current focus on "Renewable Energy" and "Sustainable Energy", this is an area that has been exploding wioth new technologies in recent years to the externt that 100% rebnewable and susteainable eleectricity generation is pretty much a reality. 
Some Countries are already there (e.g. Costa Rica, Iceland, Norway) and many more, including the UK, are on the way and the cost of renewable energy is now cheaper than fossil fuels in many parts of the world.

there are a lot of different technologies that are being developed and deployed in this area, such as...
- **Nuclear Power** allows us to generate electricity from nuclear reactions.
- **Wind Power** allows us to generate electricity from the wind.
- **Solar Power** allows us to generate electricity from the sun.
- **Wave Power** particularly useful when you live on an island.
