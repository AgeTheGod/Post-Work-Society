#   Sustainable Energy Management

**Sustainable Energy Management** is (or should be) pretty much the top priority in any modern society.  

Energy generation is a critical enabling technology (some would say the single most important requirement) and when we talk about Energy Generation we're really talking about "Electricity" because that's what powers pretty much everything that most of us care about.

Fortunately, because of the current focus on "Renewable Energy" and "Sustainable Energy", this is an area that has been exploding wioth new technologies in recent years to the externt that 100% rebnewable and susteainable eleectricity generation is pretty much a reality.
Some Countries are already there (e.g. Costa Rica, Iceland, Norway) and many more, including the UK, are on the way and the cost of renewable energy is now cheaper than fossil fuels in many parts of the world.

>    Note: I'm deliberately not addressing the Climate Change issue in this section.
>
>    Not saying that it's unimportant (it's actually a critical problem to solve) but it's something that gets resolved as a side-effect of solving the more general Energy Management problem.

##  What Is Sustainable Energy?

Sustainable Energy is essentially the generation of Electricity from resources that do not need continuous replenishment over time.

Resources in this context are not just raw materials, such as Coal and Oil and Sunshine, but also the materials used to construct whatever mechanism (the technology) is used to convert the raw materials into energy.

In an ideal (and non-existent) world this would mean technology that is maintenance free and does not degrade over time.


Even better, most of that cost is capital investment in infrastructure, i.e. initially building the things we need, and once built the on-going maintenance of renewable energy generation is significantly lower due to the durability of modern materials, such as ceramics, compared to historically significant  materials. 

##  Why Do We Need Sustainable Energy?

The global trend for energy consumption is only going one way and that is upwards.

##  Energy Management

Energy management is a three part problem...
1. How to generate Electricity from sustainable sources?
2. How to store Electricity until it is required?
3. How to distribute Electricity to where it is needed?
