#   Enabling Technologies

**Enabling Technologies** are the things that we need to have in place in order to achieve our Objectives. They are the "enabling" factors that allow us to make progress towards our goals.

What's in this section is really a survey of the significant technology that I think will play a huge part in enabling a Post-Work Society.
It's a huge area so doing a deep-dive into each of them isn't really possible so I've just summarized things instead.

A key point about everything included in this section is that it's either already here (and implemented somewhere in the world) or it's the subject of major research & development projects.

A secondary reason for this section is to highlight "**Human Ingenuity**". 
I've always found it fascinating how humans have been able to come up with all kinds of different technologies to solve all kinds of different problems.
Collectively, as a species, we seem to be very good at it and we have a long history of doing it.

So, what we have here is not a definitive list but an illustrative list of what is possible albeit with the distinct possibility that anything in this section could be replaced by something else that does the same thing but in a different, and most likely better, way in the not too distant future.

I've also tried to group things together where there are multiple technologies that roughly solve the same problem.
