### Healthcare Breakdown

For the 2024–25 financial year, its total spending was £204.7 billion with Local authorities also budgeting roughly £24.5 billion for adult social care.
The budget is broadly split into Resource (day-to-day) and Capital (investment) spending.

* Day-to-Day Spending (RDEL): £193.2 billion (94.4%) Used for staff salaries, medicines, and routine healthcare services.
    * Staff Costs: Approximately £98.5 billion.
    * Clinical Supplies/Services: ~£27.5 billion.
* Capital Spending (CDEL): £11.5 billion invested in new buildings, diagnostic equipment, and technology.
