
The biggest blockers to creating a Post Work Society are... 

- We have a political and economic system that is based on the idea of work and income and People are conditioned (through education) to believe that work is necessary for a fulfilling life and that income is the only way to measure a person's contribution to society.
- We seem to like killing each other over the things that we disagree about, which makes it difficult to have a productive conversation about how to create a better future for everyone.

##  Religion

I don't have a problem with religion per se. People need to find reasons for existence, why we are here, and what comes afterwards because otherwise the entire thing is just a big waste of time and energy. 

But I do have a problem with the way that religion is used to justify violence and to control people.
