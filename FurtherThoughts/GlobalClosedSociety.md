#   Global Closed Society

One of the main implications of a Post Work Society (and possibly it's biggest blocker to realization at a localized National Society level) is that ultimately it requires a "**Closed Society**" in order to be fully implemented.
Or, at least, that is my conclusion when thinking through to the end-game.

In this context a Closed Society is one where all the Essential Basic Services are fully provided for by the Society itself and nothing needs to be imported from external sources. 
The only kind of trading that takes place with external Societies are in non-essential Goods & Services i.e. "Luxury Goods".

the problem is that there is not a single country that is self-sufficient in all the things needed to create the Post Work Society.
This means that trading between Societies will need to take place for Essential Goods as well as Luxury Goods.

Assuming that trading will continue to be accounted for using some kind of Monetary system   

It's similar to the issues we currently have between the so-called "**Developed Nations**" and the "**Developing Nations**" only it will be orders of magnitude worse.
In fact there is a significant likelihood that we'll exterminate the entire Human Race (and everything else) before we reach a point of being able to live peacefully with each other. We already have the means to do that!

Competitive not Collaborative

This is an area where modern day Capitalism has a lot to answer for. 
Globally there's an awful lot that could be done to help the Developing Nations develop faster and catch the rest of us up.
The reason we don't do that is mostly down to the belief that "_if you want it you have to pay for it_" and a lot of things only get done if there's a commercial market where consumers pay for things.
I'll also throw systemic government corruption in there
