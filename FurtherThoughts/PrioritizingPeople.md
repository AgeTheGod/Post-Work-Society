#   Prioritizing People

Societies are made up of people and organizations, even the profit-making ones, only exist to meet the needs of those people.
This, as far as I'm concerned, is a fundamental principle of any kind of Society and, as such, means that the needs of people in a Society have priority over the needs of any organization that might operate in that society.

So, when there is a shortage of any given resource that is being utilized by both people and organizations then the needs of the people should be met first.
