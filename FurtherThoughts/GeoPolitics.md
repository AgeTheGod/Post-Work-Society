#   Geo-Political Implications



##  Not all Societies are Democracies

##  Motivations For Conflict

In my mind this is based on one of three things...
- Greed - I have something that you want and I don't want you to have it so I'm going to protect it
- Envy - You have something I want and I'm going to try and take it from you
- Fear - you scare me, usually because I don't understand you, so I'm going to protect myself from you

I could throw Ignorance in there as well but Ignorance generally translates into Fear.
