#   If Not This Then What?

"If Not This Then What?" was a common question I discussed widely with people I worked with over the years.
It's the simple question of if I didn;t do what I was currently doing then what would I be doing instead?

As you get older it becomes a progressively more important question when deciding whether to carry on with the daily grind or go off and do something else with what remains of your life.

It's a critical question when dealing with the idea of a Post Work Society because if the daily grind is not the main consumer of someone's time then what would people do with all the additional time they have?

Like all of these kinds of question there isn't a single simple answer and the answer that most people will give is heavily dependent on what each person thinks is a worthwhile use of their time.

Funny enough, now that I'm a member of the traditional Post Work Society (i.e. retired from the daily grind) I've become very aware of all the things I can do to fill in the time.
Some of those things are purely personal and some are helpful to other people and some are actually beneficial to my local community.
