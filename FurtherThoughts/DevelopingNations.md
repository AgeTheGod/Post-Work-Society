#  Developing Nations

It's similar to the issues we currently have between the so-called "**Developed Nations**" and the "**Developing Nations**" only it will be orders of magnitude worse.
In fact there is a significant likelihood that we'll exterminate the entire Human Race (and everything else) before we reach a point of being able to live peacefully with each other. We already have the means to do that!

Competitive not Collaborative

This is an area where modern day Capitalism has a lot to answer for.
Globally there's an awful lot that could be done to help the Developing Nations develop faster and catch the rest of us up.
The reason we don't do that is mostly down to the belief that "_if you want it you have to pay for it_" and a lot of things only get done if there's a commercial market where consumers pay for things.
