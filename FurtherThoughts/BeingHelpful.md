

Like all of these kinds of question there isn't a single simple answer and the answer that most people will give is heavily dependent on how they view other people (and very rarely view themselves).

At one end if the spectrum we have the pessimistic view of people being fundamentally lazy and will just lay around doing absolutely nothing unless they are forced to "work".
In this view everyone is horrible and nasty, selfish and mean to everyone else.
In the extreme, it portrays everyone in a overall negative way that leaves very little hope for a better future because people are just not capable of sharing and living peacefully with other people.

At the other end we have the optimistic view of people as being essentially caring and helpful

I think the truth lies somewhere in between
