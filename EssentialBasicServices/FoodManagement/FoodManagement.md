#   Food Management

The main problem with Food Management is that as of 2026 no Country, other than Guyana, is self-sufficient in Food Production.
There are multiple reasons for this, such as...
- Global Population Growth
- Not all Land is Arable Land
- Arable Land is good Building Land
- Preferences change over time
- Food Ethics in Society

Hence Food Management is not just about Domestic Food Production (what is produced within a Society) but also Food Trading (buying to fill a shortage) and Food Distribution (getting the Food to where its needed).
