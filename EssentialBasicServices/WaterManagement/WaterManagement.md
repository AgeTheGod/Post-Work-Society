#   Water Management

Everyone know that Water is critical to life without it the averge person would die in a matter of days. So, this obviously qualifies as an essential service.

However, even though Water is vitally important the quality of available water is very low and, according to the United Nations, is a severe planetary crisis
Globally, the United Nations reports that over 80% of the world's wastewater flows back into the ecosystem without being treated or reused.

In addition Water Management Technology has lagged significantly behind other technology.

### Desalination

Desalinatio is the solution of chpoice in many countries that have water scarcity and a significant coastline.

China just solved its water scarcity problem by building the largest solar-powered desalination network along their eastern seaboard.
The system pulls the ocean in. It uses massive coastal solar farms to strip the salt away. 
It pumps clean drinking water inland at a scale never before attempted. 

The historical challenge with desalination has always been the power bill. 
Pushing ocean water through reverse osmosis membranes requires colossal amounts of energy, which traditionally meant burning immense quantities of fossil fuels just to get a glass of clean water.
By hardwiring these massive desalination plants directly into dedicated coastal solar infrastructure, they bypassed the core flaw of the technology. The energy is free and carbon-neutral.

The next global engineering hurdle is the brine—the hyper-salty byproduct of the process. 
But even that is shifting from a waste problem to a resource, with modern facilities increasingly extracting commercially vital minerals like lithium and magnesium from the discharge before it is diluted and released.
